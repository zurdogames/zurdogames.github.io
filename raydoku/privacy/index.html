Raydoku: Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Raydoku is a puzzle game made by Zurdo Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email us at zurdogamesstudio@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. Cloud save through Google Play Games is optional.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Remove Ads or Raydoku Complete turns off ads between levels.
  • We use Google Firebase to see how the game is played and to receive crash reports.
  • We don't sell your information.

Information stored on your phone

Your puzzle progress, Daily results, hints, purchases and settings are stored on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Cloud save (optional)

If you connect Google Play Games under Settings → Cloud saves, the game saves a copy of your progress to your Google account, so you can continue on another phone. This copy is stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can pause cloud saves in Settings, and delete the saved copy from your Google Play Games settings.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional ad between levels, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads, hint packs, the Prism Expansion and Raydoku Complete) are made through Google Play. Google handles payment, and we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid and to restore it on a new phone. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and your purchase records for this game, not your name or email. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Gameplay analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example which levels people finish, how long a puzzle takes and how often hints are used. Firebase uses an app-instance identifier and may receive device and app information and your approximate country (derived from your IP address). It does not collect your advertising ID, and we don't use this data for advertising. We use it only to improve the game. See Firebase's privacy information.

Crash reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details: what went wrong in the game's code, the app version, your phone model and Android version, and an installation identifier. It doesn't include your name or email.

Notifications

If you turn on puzzle notifications, the reminders are scheduled on your phone. No information is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game or in your phone's notification settings.

Sharing

When you share a result, your phone's share menu sends it only where you choose.

Children

Raydoku is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you uninstall the game. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. To ask us to delete information linked to your device, such as analytics, crash reports or your purchase records with RevenueCat, email zurdogamesstudio@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Email us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
