Raydoku: Support

Light, mirrors and logic. We're happy to help.

Contact us

Email zurdogamesstudio@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, Android version and what happened, and we'll reply as soon as we can, usually within a few days.

How do I play?

Place mirrors so every lamp's beam reaches its matching lens, using the number clues on each row and column. You can replay the tutorial any time from Settings → Help → How to Play.

I bought something but don't have it

Open Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account you bought with. If a payment was still processing, close and reopen the game, then restore again. Still missing? Email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.), found in your Google Play purchase history.

My hints didn't come back after a restore

Restore brings back permanent unlocks (Remove Ads, the Prism Expansion, Raydoku Complete). Hint balances move between phones through cloud saves, and hints you've already used aren't given again.

Moving to a new phone

On your old phone, open Settings → Cloud saves, connect Google Play Games and wait for "Saved with Play Games". On the new phone, install Raydoku, connect the same Play Games account and your progress comes across. Without cloud saves, progress stays only on the old phone.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google. You can request one from your Google Play order history. See Google Play's refund policy.

Ads and privacy choices

Remove Ads or Raydoku Complete turns off ads between levels; ads you choose to watch for a reward stay optional. Where the law requires it, you can change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices. Our Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes the progress on your phone. You can delete your cloud save from your Google Play Games settings. To ask us to delete analytics, crash reports or purchase records linked to your device, email zurdogamesstudio@gmail.com.

I found a puzzle that seems wrong

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, but if one looks broken, email us the chapter and level number (or the Daily date) and a screenshot. We'll look into it.
