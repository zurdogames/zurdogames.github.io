Nonogram Town: Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 (updated: usage data is optional, and Android backup)

Nonogram Town is a puzzle game made by Zurdo Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email us at zurdogamesstudio@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. Cloud save through Google Play Games is optional.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Premium turns off the ads between puzzles.
  • We use Google Firebase to receive crash reports and, if you allow it, to see how the game is played. You can turn usage data off any time in Settings.
  • We don't sell your information.

Information stored on your phone

Your puzzles, towns, Daily results, hints, purchases and settings are stored on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them from the phone.

If your phone's Google backup is on, Android includes the game's save file in it, so your progress can come back when you reinstall or move to a new phone. That backup is made by Android and stored in your Google account under Google's Privacy Policy; we never receive it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Cloud save (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game saves a copy of your progress to your Google account, so you can continue on another phone. This copy is stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can delete the saved copy from your Google Play Games settings.

While you're signed in, the game also sends your achievements and leaderboard scores (puzzles solved, your best daily streak and your Daily times) to Google Play Games. Leaderboards show your Play Games name and score to other players, as your Play Games profile settings allow.

Progress backup and sharing

Export progress in Settings writes your progress to a file that you choose where to keep; Import progress reads one back. Share picture makes an image of a puzzle you solved. These go only where you send them.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, your phone shows a notification on days your Daily puzzle is waiting. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us. Turn it off in Settings or your phone's notification settings.

Daily puzzles

To get new Daily puzzles, the game downloads a puzzle file from our content host (GitHub Pages). Like any website, the host sees your IP address and basic device information when the file is fetched. Nothing about you or your progress is sent.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional ad between puzzles, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward such as hints. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases (Premium and premium worlds) are made through Google Play. Google handles payment, and we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid and to restore it on a new phone. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and your purchase records for this game, not your name or email. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Gameplay analytics

With your permission, we use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example which puzzles people finish, how long a puzzle takes and how often hints are used. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, nothing is collected unless you turn on Settings → Share anonymous usage data. Elsewhere it is on unless you turn that switch off. Firebase uses an app-instance identifier and may receive device and app information and your approximate country (derived from your IP address). It does not collect your advertising ID, and we don't use this data for advertising. We use it only to improve the game. See Firebase's privacy information.

Crash reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details: what went wrong in the game's code, the app version, your phone model and Android version, and an installation identifier. It doesn't include your name or email.

Children

Nonogram Town is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you uninstall the game. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. To ask us to delete information linked to your device, such as analytics, crash reports or your purchase records with RevenueCat, email zurdogamesstudio@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Email us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
