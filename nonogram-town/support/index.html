Nonogram Town: Support

Solve a picture, build a town. We're happy to help.

Contact us

Email zurdogamesstudio@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, Android version and what happened, and we'll reply as soon as we can, usually within a few days.

How do I play?

Each puzzle hides a picture. The numbers beside each row and above each column are its clues: each number is a run of filled cells, in the order shown, with at least one empty cell between runs. Tap a cell to fill it, or hold and drag to fill a line. Switch to Cross to mark cells you know are empty. Nothing is checked as you go: use Check to find mistakes and Undo to take moves back.

Hints and Reveal

Hint points you to a row or column whose clue tells you something new; it never fills cells for you. Reveal uses a hint to show the answer for one cell you tap. When you run out, you can watch a short ad for 3 more hints. Premium gives unlimited hints.

Building your town

Every puzzle you solve unlocks an object for your town. After a puzzle, the town opens with the new object over its glowing plot: tap the plot (or Place) to set it down. Drag to look round, pinch or use + and − to zoom, tap a building to see its name, and use Visit a place to jump to anything you've built.

The Daily puzzle

There's a new Daily puzzle every day, the same one for every player. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak: 7, 14 and 30 days in a row each earn hints. Turn on Settings → Daily reminder for one gentle note in the evening while today's puzzle is waiting.

I bought something but don't have it

Open Shop → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account you bought with. If a payment was still processing, close and reopen the game, then restore again. Still missing? Email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.), found in your Google Play purchase history.

Moving to a new phone

On your old phone, open Settings → Google Play Games and sign in. On the new phone, install Nonogram Town and sign in to the same Play Games account, and your progress comes across. You can also use Settings → Export progress to save your progress to a file, then Import progress on the new phone. Purchases come back with Restore purchases.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google. You can request one from your Google Play order history. See Google Play's refund policy.

Ads and privacy choices

Premium turns off ads between puzzles; ads you choose to watch for hints stay optional. Where the law requires it, you can change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy options. Our Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes the progress on your phone. You can delete your cloud save from your Google Play Games settings. To ask us to delete analytics, crash reports or purchase records linked to your device, email zurdogamesstudio@gmail.com.

Something isn't working

  • The town feels slow: close other apps and restart the game. Settings → Reduce motion also lightens the town's animations.
  • No sound: check Settings → Music and Sound, and your phone's volume.
  • The Daily didn't change: new Daily puzzles download when you open the game, so check your connection and reopen it.

I found a puzzle that seems wrong

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, but if one looks broken, email us the world and puzzle number (or the Daily date) and a screenshot. We'll look into it.
