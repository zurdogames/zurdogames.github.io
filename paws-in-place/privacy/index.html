Paws in Place: Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Paws in Place is a puzzle game made by Zurdo Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email us at sivareno12@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which uses device information to show and measure ads. Premium removes ads.
  • We don't sell your information.

Information stored on your phone

Your puzzle progress, coins, rewards, room decorations and settings are stored on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If you choose Export backup, the game creates a file of your progress and you choose where to save or share it.

Cloud save (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game can save a copy of your progress to your Google account, so you can continue on another phone. This copy is stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can delete it from your Google Play Games settings.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional ad between puzzles, and optional ads you can watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Purchases & privacy → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles payment, and we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check that your purchase is valid and to restore it on a new phone. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and your purchase records, not your name or email. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, it sends a crash report to Sentry so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details only: what went wrong in the game's code, the app version, your phone model and Android version. It doesn't include your name, email or advertising ID. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your phone. No information is sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Sharing and ratings

When you share a win card, your phone's share menu sends it only where you choose. If the game asks for a rating, the rating is handled by Google Play.

Children

Paws in Place is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you uninstall the game. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. To ask us to delete information linked to your device, such as crash reports or your purchase records with RevenueCat, email sivareno12@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Email us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
